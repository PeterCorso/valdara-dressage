Stallion
Stock

Approved Bloodlines

Our stallions are selected for rideability, correct conformation, and three pure, elastic gaits — then proven under saddle before they ever stand at stud. Each carries approvals with the leading European warmblood studbooks.

Breeding Services

Fresh-cooled and frozen semen, on-site live cover by arrangement, and embryo transfer coordinated with our reproductive veterinarians. We advise on every cross — not just the ones that use our stallions.
