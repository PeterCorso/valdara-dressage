Our Horses

Approved stallions, proven mares, and the next generation — each selected for temperament, soundness, and three pure gaits.

Stallion
Stock

Selected, Then Proven

A Valdara stallion must pass his studbook approval, complete performance testing, and show under saddle before he is offered to outside mares. Temperament is weighed as heavily as movement.

Breeding Available

Fresh-cooled and frozen semen ship nationwide. Mare owners receive a pre-breeding consultation and a cross analysis from our breeding manager at no additional cost.
