Questions

Lesson FAQ

What should I wear to my first lesson?

Breeches or riding tights, tall or paddock boots with a heel, gloves, and an ASTM/SEI-certified helmet. Loaner helmets are available for evaluation lessons.

Do you teach complete beginners?

We welcome riders who have basic walk-trot-canter skills. If you are brand new to riding, we can recommend a lunge-line foundation series before joining the program.

What is your cancellation policy?

Lessons cancelled with 24 hours' notice can be rescheduled at no charge. Weather cancellations are always free.

Can I haul in my own horse?

Yes. Haul-in lessons are welcome Tuesday through Saturday. Please bring a current negative Coggins and vaccination record on your first visit.
